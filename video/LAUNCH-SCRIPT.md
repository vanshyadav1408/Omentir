# Omentir launch video: 15 seconds, shooting script

Format: 1920 x 1080, 30 fps, 15.00 seconds, 450 frames. Landscape. No captions burned in besides the supers already in the cut. URL is on screen only. Never spoken.

The current Remotion composition is 390 frames (13.00s). Hold the last card to frame 450 so the guarantee has room. Do not speed up the product, leads, or inbox scenes.

This cut is a product loop, not a feature tour. Picture does the detail. Voice names the job, the motion, the price, and the refund.

---

## Voiceover (record this)

Record one take, dry, no music in the cans. Leave 0.3s of silence at the head and 0.4s at the tail.

Omentir will find you customers. Describe your product. It finds matching leads on LinkedIn, sends invites and follow-ups from your account, and books meetings into one inbox. Forty-nine dollars a month. Three bookings a week, or you pay nothing.

42 words. Speak it in 14.6 to 15.0 seconds. If a take runs past 15.2, it is too slow. If it lands under 13.8, you swallowed a pause.

### Breath marks

Read it like this. A slash is a short catch-breath. A double slash is a beat, about the length of a comma you can hear.

Omentir will find you **customers.** //
Describe your product. /
It finds matching leads on LinkedIn, /
sends invites and follow-ups from your account, /
and books meetings into one inbox. //
Forty-nine dollars a month. /
Three bookings a week, / or you pay **nothing.**

Stress: customers, nothing. Do not stress Omentir, LinkedIn, forty-nine, or inbox. Those are facts, not punchlines.

### How to say each line

**Omentir will find you customers.**
Say it as a promise you are willing to stand behind, because the close repeats the cost of failing it. Slight downward finish on customers. Do not grin it. Do not make it sound like a tagline read.

**Describe your product.**
Shorter. Instructional. You are pointing at the form on screen. No extra warmth.

**It finds matching leads on LinkedIn,**
"It" is Omentir. Keep the comma. This clause is still going. Eyes are on the scored list.

**sends invites and follow-ups from your account,**
"Your account" is the point. LinkedIn is already in the last clause, so do not say LinkedIn again. "Invites and follow-ups" is what the inbox thread is about to prove. Keep it even. Do not sell "automation."

**and books meetings into one inbox.**
Land on inbox, then stop. This is the last product sentence. Give it the double slash. The meeting-booked badge should already be on Elena Voss by the time you finish "inbox."

**Forty-nine dollars a month.**
Flat. Almost throwaway. Do not make price sound like a bargain or a flex. Say forty-nine, not 49, not forty nine without the hyphen in your mouth. "Dollars a month" in full. No "only." No "just."

**Three bookings a week, or you pay nothing.**
This is the homepage line split across two breaths. "Three bookings a week" is the bar. "Or you pay nothing" is the refund. Do not smile. Do not wink. It is a rule. End on nothing and stop. Do not add omentir.com. Do not add "get started."

### What not to do in the read

Do not do an announcer voice.
Do not speed up to cram extra product facts.
Do not say "AI" out loud in this cut. The product UI is the proof.
Do not say "SDR," "ICP," "MCP," "self-host," or "open source." Picture cannot support those claims in 15 seconds, and the VO will clip.
Do not say "seamless," "unlock," "empower," or "game changer."
Do not ad-lib "no credit card" or "free trial." Those are not in this script.

## What the 15 seconds is allowed to claim

Say or show only what the site already states.

You may say: Omentir will find you customers. You describe your product. It finds matching leads on LinkedIn. It sends invites and follow-ups from your account. It books meetings into one inbox. Pro is forty-nine dollars a month. Three bookings a week, or you pay nothing.

You may show: My Product fields, LinkedIn connected, ICP chips, profile scan counts, fit scores, low-fit skipped, invite / message / replied steps, a real-looking thread, a meeting booked state, the homepage CTA.

You may not say in this cut: open source, self-host, Docker, MCP, Grok Bot, Claude, ChatGPT, API keys, Enterprise, SSO, unlimited agents, human-in-the-loop review queue, daily invite caps, Unipile, Firebase. Those are true of the product. They belong in a 45-second cut or a landing page, not here.

You may not say: we replace your SDR team, we log into Sales Navigator, we send without you, we guarantee revenue, we scrape anyone on LinkedIn.

## Record checklist

- One voice, close mic, dry room, 48 kHz.
- Peak around -6 dB. No limiter pumping.
- Watch the picture while you record. If you look at a script only, you will miss the Elena pill.
- Three usable takes of the full 15 seconds. Do not punch in a single word unless "nothing" clips.
- Play it back once with the sound off and confirm the picture still makes sense. Then play it with VO and confirm you are not naming a screen the viewer cannot see yet.
- Last frame: logo, or you pay nothing, both buttons, omentir.com, all readable on a phone in landscape.
